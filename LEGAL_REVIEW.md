# Fosko Web Legal Review

Review first. Do not modify code unless explicitly asked.

This is a review framework, not a substitute for qualified legal counsel where the matter is material or uncertain.

## 1. Privacy and personal information
Check actual personal information collection, privacy-policy consistency, analytics/tracking, cookies/storage, third-party services, inquiry handling, and disclosure/correction/suspension/deletion/complaint procedures where applicable.

## 2. Corporate information
Check accuracy and consistency of company name, English name, business description, contact channel, corporate number if displayed, and location description.
Do not require unnecessary disclosure of a private residential address when another lawful publication method is available.

## 3. Website representations
Check for misleading claims, guarantees, unsupported performance claims, customer/partner/certification/affiliation claims, IP concerns, and wording that may create unintended contractual expectations.

## 4. Terms and user interactions
Assess whether new features create additional obligations: contact forms, accounts, payments, subscriptions, e-commerce, UGC, downloads, services, marketing email, recruitment.

## 5. Third-party services
Review services actually used, including Google Fonts, Cloudflare Web Analytics, and GitHub Pages.

## Classification
- **LEGAL BLOCKER**
- **SHOULD FIX**
- **OPTIONAL**
- **NO ISSUE**

For every LEGAL BLOCKER or SHOULD FIX:
- Exact page / section
- Concern
- Whether it is a clear requirement or risk-reduction recommendation
- Minimum wording or implementation change

Do not invent legal requirements. State uncertainty explicitly.
